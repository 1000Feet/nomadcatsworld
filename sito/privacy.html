Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This page explains what the Nomad Cats World app uses, what it shares and what it does not.

Who we are

Nomad Cats World is made by 1000 Feet, Inc. You can write to us at angelo@angelomagni.it.

Your location

The app uses your location to show you the cats near you.

If you allow location access "Always", the app also uses it to tell you when a cat is within about 100 metres, even when the app is closed.

What other players can see

When you set a cat free, these are shared with other players:

  • the cat's name;
  • the cat's passport: the places and dates of its adoptions;
  • the spot where you leave the cat.

When you adopt a cat, its number and the name you give it are written in a public register of all 21,000,000 cats.

Camera

The camera is used to show the cat in your room. Photos you take stay on your phone.

Microphone

The microphone is used only to hear that you are calling a cat. Nothing is recorded.

Your cats

The cats you adopt are saved on your phone and in your own private iCloud.

What the app does not have

There is no account to create. There is no advertising and there are no analytics.

Purchases

The only purchase in the app is the Nomad Pass subscription. It is handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details.

Deleting your data

To ask us to delete your data, write to angelo@angelomagni.it.
