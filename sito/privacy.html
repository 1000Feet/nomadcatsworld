This website

The website has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It sets no cookies. The fonts and images are served from the site itself, not from other companies.

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. in the United States. Like any web host, Netlify receives your IP address and basic technical details of each request (such as the browser and the page asked for) and keeps short-lived logs to deliver the site and keep it secure.

The waiting list

If you leave your email address in the waiting-list form, we keep that address and the time you sent it. We use it for one thing: to write to you once, on the day the app opens. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box in the form.

  • The form is handled by Netlify, which stores the submissions for us.
  • We do not sell, rent or share the list, and we do not use it for anything else.
  • We delete the list after that email has been sent, and in any case within twelve months of the day you signed up.
  • You can ask us to remove your address at any time by writing to info@1000feetabove.com.

The app

The app is in testing. This is how it works today.

  • Location. While you play, your location stays on your phone. It is used there to show the cats near you and to tell you when one is close.
  • Camera. The camera stays on your phone. It is used to place a cat in your room in augmented reality and to take a photo when you ask for one. Photos are saved on your phone.
  • Microphone. The microphone stays on your phone. It only measures how loud the sound is, to hear that you are calling a cat. Nothing is recorded.
  • Your adopted cats. The cats you adopt are saved on your phone and in your own private iCloud, which is run by Apple under your Apple account.

There is no account to create and there are no analytics. The app uses the iCloud account already on your phone.

When you set a cat free

This is the one moment the app shares something. When you set a cat free, three things go into a public database, where other players nearby can see them:

  • the spot where you leave the cat;
  • the cat's name;
  • the cat's passport: the places and dates of its adoptions.

Because other players can see this, choose a name that says nothing personal about you, and set a cat free somewhere you are happy for others to see. Your location at any other time is not shared.

Children

The website and the waiting list are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their email addresses. If you believe a child has signed up, write to us and we will delete the address.
